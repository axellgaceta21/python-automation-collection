Business Lead Scraping Automation

Turn website sources into organized business information.

03 / The workflow

How it works

  1. Load website sources

    Read the business website list and its optional context fields.

  2. Visit relevant pages

    Request websites and selected internal pages for useful business content.

  3. Extract and organize

    Collect contact and business information, and record URL processing issues.

  4. Create the report

    Export structured business records and invalid URL findings.
